# Prymm Enterprises redesign

## Emotional brief
A professional, corporate, vibrant system should create immediate trust, authority, and energetic innovation. Navy provides reliability; electric blue signals competence; cyan adds technical freshness; amber directs attention; lime adds controlled momentum.

## Experience philosophy
The page behaves like a global trade pavilion: structural scale first, then clear sector wayfinding, proof, and a focused quote action. It should feel as precise as a medical laboratory, as engineered as a manufacturing plant, and as frictionless as a global logistics hub.

## Typography and interaction
Strong condensed display typography represents heavy machinery while crisp labels and clear body text reflect medical and digital precision. Card lifts and button movements are small, snappy, and purposeful. Focus states remain visible and reduced-motion users receive the same information without animation.

## Emotional journey
Begin with vibrant capability, transition through confidence-building product sectors, and culminate in a trust-driven quote request. Color is deployed as information architecture rather than decoration.
