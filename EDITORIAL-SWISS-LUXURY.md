# Editorial Swiss luxury direction

The landing page blends Editorial, Swiss/International, Luxury Minimal, and Tech Forward principles into one cohesive scrolling narrative.

- Editorial: serif headlines, measured pacing, and a magazine-like sense of hierarchy.
- Swiss/International: disciplined grid, restrained rules, concise labels, and functional navigation.
- Luxury Minimal: warm obsidian/alabaster surfaces, champagne gold, generous whitespace, and no unnecessary decoration.
- Tech Forward: dark-mode support, precise interaction states, accessible responsive behavior, and a cinematic focal image.

The emotional arc progresses from capability to discovery, confidence, and an intentional sourcing conversation. The page preserves verified facts and avoids unsupported claims or invented contact workflows.
