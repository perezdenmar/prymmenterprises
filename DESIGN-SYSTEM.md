# Selected design direction

The redesign combines **Modernist**, **Corporate Professional**, **Tech Forward**, and **Luxury Minimal**, with restrained glassmorphism used only for depth.

- Modernist: disciplined grid, functional hierarchy, generous whitespace, no decorative noise.
- Corporate Professional: navy, steel, factual copy, confident wayfinding, and clear next actions.
- Tech Forward: cyan and electric-blue signals, precise motion, system-aware dark mode, and responsive behavior.
- Luxury Minimal: fewer elements, stronger typography, translucent panels, soft shadows, and premium restraint.

The emotional arc is capability on arrival, clarity through sectors, confidence through proof, and secure momentum at the quote action. The page avoids unsupported claims, competitor references, and invented contact details.
